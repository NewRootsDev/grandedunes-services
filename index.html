Services

Residential site plan drafting

Pool, addition, outdoor kitchen, hardscape, fence and drainage exhibits drafted from your surveyor's boundary and topographic data. Setbacks, easements, impervious area and drainage shown the way the reviewers expect to see them.

Drone imagery and progress documentation

Dated aerial imagery of a custom home or renovation from foundation to finish, flown under LAANC authorization in the controlled airspace over this part of the city by an FAA Part 107 remote pilot.

As-built and record drawings

Drawings of what was actually built, from your surveyor's as-built data or from field measurement, for closeout, resale files and future work on the property.

Landscape and hardscape base plans

Accurate base drawings for landscape designers and pool contractors, with existing trees, grades, utilities and structures located so the design fits the lot.

Quantity takeoffs for builders

Earthwork, paving, drainage and hardscape quantities from the plan, itemized for pricing and for checking a subcontractor's bid.

Submittal review

A completeness check of your package against the architectural review requirements and the City of Myrtle Beach checklist before you submit, so the first review is not spent on missing items.

Questions we get

Can you do the survey for my pool permit?

No. A boundary or topographic survey that carries a seal comes from a licensed South Carolina land surveyor. We draft the site plan exhibit from that survey and coordinate with the surveyor directly if you like.

Do you know the Grande Dunes review requirements?

We work from the published requirements and the City of Myrtle Beach checklist, and we check your package against them. Interpretation and approval belong to the review board and the city.

Can you fly a drone over my house?

Yes, with your permission as the owner or your builder's. This part of the city is inside controlled airspace for Myrtle Beach International Airport, so flights are scheduled under LAANC authorization.

What do builders usually order?

A site plan exhibit for permitting, progress imagery at a few milestones, and an as-built drawing at the end. Each can be ordered on its own.

Do you work outside Grande Dunes?

Yes. The same services are available across Myrtle Beach and the Grand Strand through New Roots Development, LLC.
